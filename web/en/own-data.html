When it helps

{{t:web.brand}} downloads the public elevation data of each country itself. Some data can't be downloaded for you: they need your own registration, a permit or a purchase, and the licence is yours. You can still use them for your map: download them yourself, crop them to your area and upload them with the map order. The same works for your own data, for example from a drone survey or from your municipality.

Where such data come from

Examples. The licence is always that of the provider; check it before you upload.

  • Slovenia – national LiDAR (ARSO / GURS), point cloud and terrain model by tiles.
  • Finland – dense laser scanning 5 p/m² of the National Land Survey (MML) needs a permit and may be used only inside the permit holder's organisation. Upload it only for your own (club) map.
  • Wallonia (Belgium) – SPW elevation models, published only as large province archives.
  • Hungary, Croatia – national LiDAR is sold; the licence belongs to the buyer.
  • Scotland – some LiDAR phases have a non-commercial licence only.
  • Anywhere – a drone survey, data from a local authority or a forest owner.

What to upload

  • Two files: terrain (DTM, bare ground) and surface (DSM, including trees and buildings). Vegetation is computed from their difference, so both are needed.
  • Each one a single GeoTIFF (.tif) with one band of heights in metres and a NoData value. No VRT, ZIP or side files.
  • A projected coordinate system in metres (for example UTM or the national grid), stored in the file, best with an EPSG code. Not degrees, not rotated.
  • Resolution 0.1 to 5 m per pixel, covering at least 90 % of the map area.
  • At most {{MAX_MB}} MB both files together, which is why the data must be cropped to the area.

How to crop the data to your area

QGIS

  1. Open the raster and zoom to your map area (with a small margin).
  2. Raster → Extraction → Clip Raster by Extent, set the extent to the map canvas and save as GeoTIFF.
  3. Do the same for the second file.

GDAL command line

The upload box on the main page shows this command with your current area already filled in (W S E N are longitude and latitude in degrees): 

gdalwarp -te_srs EPSG:4326 -te W S E N -co COMPRESS=DEFLATE -co TILED=YES dtm_source.tif dtm.tif
gdalwarp -te_srs EPSG:4326 -te W S E N -co COMPRESS=DEFLATE -co TILED=YES dsm_source.tif dsm.tif

From a point cloud (LAS / LAZ)

If you only have a point cloud, make the two rasters first, for example in QGIS 3.32 or newer (Processing → Point cloud conversion → Export to raster (using triangulation)): terrain from ground points only (classification 2), surface from all points, resolution 0.5 to 1 m.

What happens to your data

  • A map from your own data is always private; the download link comes by e-mail.
  • The files are checked in an isolated process, cropped to your area and used only for this map.
  • They are never cached, never shown to anyone and never used for another map. They are deleted when the map is finished, even if it fails.
  • By uploading you confirm that you have the right to use the data and hand them over for processing.
