ČÚZK data

The digital terrain model DMR 5G, the surface model DMP OK and the topographic database ZABAGED® are provided by the Land Survey Office / ČÚZK (Czech Office for Surveying, Mapping and Cadastre) as open data under CC BY 4.0.

  • ČÚZK terms for providing spatial data (in Czech)

On printed output: ČÚZK, [year] (year by how current the data are).

Regional DTM (DMVS)

Surveyed features (sidewalks, roads, buildings, stairs, fences, bridges…) come from the regional digital technical map, maintained by the regions and published by ČÚZK in the DMVS information system (public ZPS datasets). The public data are available free of charge and without registration (“without any conditions”); the DMVS information system was built with IROP funding (co-financed by the European Union).

No explicit licence (such as CC BY) is stated for these datasets, and the ČÚZK terms (CC BY 4.0, “ČÚZK, [year]”) do not explicitly cover the regional DTM. Use of the data in Podkladárna outputs has, however, been confirmed. We credit the source in the spirit of those terms: Regional DTM · ČÚZK / DMVS, [year].

  • DMVS – data provision (in Czech)
  • DMVS information system project (IROP, in Czech)

Sentinel-2 satellite images

The optional template references/sentinel2_*.jpg comes from the Copernicus programme (ESA / EU); Sentinel-2 L2A data are free to use with attribution and are downloaded from AWS Open Data (Element84 earth-search). They are not used to draw the map. Credit: Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data [year].

Inspiration

Podkladárna was loosely inspired by the Karttapullautin project and then went its own way: vegetation from LiDAR return density, cliffs and rocks from the DMR, GDAL contours.

Symbol sets

The ISOM 2017-2 (Revision 6) and ISSprOM 2019-2 (Revision 6) sets are based on the official OCAD symbol sets; the basic symbol names follow the official Czech translations of the specifications (ČSOS), sub-symbol names are derived. Likewise, the ISMTBOM 2022 set for MTBO is based on the official OCAD set; names of symbols without an official Czech translation are derived.
